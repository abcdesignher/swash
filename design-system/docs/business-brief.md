# SWASH BUSINESS BRIEF

**A simpler, smarter laundry experience for Nigeria.**

### 1. THE BUSINESS

Swash is a subscription-led laundry service in Nigeria serving individuals, families, and small businesses. Its mobile app brings laundry orders, payments, subscriptions, and collection or delivery arrangements into one convenient experience, supported by an AI assistant.

### 2. THE CUSTOMER

**Primary audience:** Busy Nigerian professionals aged 25–40.

- **Device:** Primarily Android smartphones.
- **Connectivity:** Mobile data or Wi-Fi that may be slow or unreliable.
- **Technical ability:** Moderate; expects a familiar, intuitive experience without a learning curve.
- **Core need:** Manage laundry, understand costs, and get reliable updates without repeatedly contacting staff.

### 3. THE NON-NEGOTIABLE

**Accurate, connected customer records.**

Every order must link correctly to its payment, subscription allowance, and current status. The customer app, staff system, and AI assistant must reflect the same information. Without this foundation, the service cannot earn customer trust.

### 4. THE AI ADVANTAGE

Using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), the assistant answers questions using approved business information and authenticated access to customers’ own records.

It must reliably answer:

- **Pricing:** How much does it cost to wash my duvet?
- **Subscriptions:** Can I roll over my subscription, and how much allowance do I have left?
- **Orders:** What is the status of my laundry, and when can I collect it?

The assistant must protect customer privacy, acknowledge missing information, and never invent details or claim actions have succeeded without system confirmation.

### 5. THE DESIGN DIRECTION

**Simple. Familiar. Reassuring. Slightly expressive.**

- **Colours:** Rich brown and blue, inspired by their combination in Nigerian Ankara fabrics, for subtle cultural familiarity.
- **Typography:** Open Sans for readability; Playfair Display for expressive, distinctive headings.
- **Experience:** Mobile-first, lightweight, easy to navigate, and resilient to unreliable internet connections.

### SUCCESS PRINCIPLE

Make laundry effortless for customers without making daily operations harder for the business. Every design decision must reinforce clarity, reliability, and trust.
