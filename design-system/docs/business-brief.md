# SWASH BUSINESS BRIEF

**A simpler, smarter laundry experience for Nigeria.**

### 1. THE BUSINESS
Swash is a subscription-led laundry service in Nigeria serving individuals, families, and small businesses. Its web app brings laundry orders, payments, subscriptions, and collection or delivery arrangements into one convenient experience, supported by an AI assistant.

### 2. THE CUSTOMER
**Primary audience:** Busy Nigerian professionals aged 25–40.

- **Device:** Primarily laptops and desktop computers.
- **Connectivity:** Uses mobile data or Wi-Fi, which may occasionally be slow or unreliable.
- **Technical ability:** Comfortable with basic computer operations and familiar web interfaces.
- **Core need:** Manage laundry, understand costs, and get reliable updates without repeatedly contacting staff.

### 3. THE NON-NEGOTIABLE

**Accurate, connected customer records.**
Every order must link correctly to its payment, subscription allowance, and current status. The customer-facing web app, staff system, and AI assistant must reflect the same information. Without this foundation, the service cannot earn customer trust.

### 4. THE AI ADVANTAGE
Using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), the assistant answers questions using approved business information and authenticated access to customers’ own records.

It must reliably answer:

- **Pricing:** How much does it cost to wash my duvet?
- **Subscriptions:** Can I roll over my subscription, and how much allowance do I have left?
- **Orders:** What is the status of my laundry, and when can I collect it?

The assistant must protect customer privacy, acknowledge missing information, and never invent details or claim actions have succeeded without confirmation from the relevant system.

### 5. THE DESIGN DIRECTION

**Simple. Familiar. Reassuring. Slightly expressive.**
- **Colours:** Rich brown/gold and deep violet/purple, inspired by their combination in Nigerian Ankara fabrics, for subtle cultural familiarity.
- **Typography:** Open Sans for readability; Playfair for expressive, distinctive headings.
- **Experience:** A clear, responsive web interface with intuitive navigation, readable layouts, lightweight pages, and helpful loading or retry states for unreliable connections.

### SUCCESS PRINCIPLE
Make laundry effortless for customers without making daily operations harder for the business. Every design decision must reinforce clarity, reliability, cultural relevance, and trust.
