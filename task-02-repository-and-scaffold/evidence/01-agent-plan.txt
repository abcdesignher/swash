2. Summary of Understanding
Product & Audience: Swash is a subscription-led laundry service in Nigeria serving individuals, families, and small businesses. Its primary audience is busy Nigerian professionals aged 25–40 who primarily use laptops/desktops and may experience slow or unreliable internet connections.
Core Principle: Accurate, connected customer records across the customer web application, staff system, and RAG-powered AI assistant.
Mandatory Technology Stack: Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Prisma ORM, and PostgreSQL.
Design System Contract:
06-tokens-complete.json is the single source of truth, converted into CSS custom properties in global.css.
Colours: Primitive variables (--primitive-colour-*) define the palette; semantic reference roles (--colour-*) define usage. Interface styles must exclusively consume semantic reference roles and never reference primitive variables or hardcoded colour values.
Typography: 24 exported styles across Open Sans (body, labels, UI controls) and Playfair / Playfair Display (expressive display, headline, and title styles).
Effects: Three brand-tinted elevation shadows (--shadow-soft-primary, --shadow-medium-primary, --shadow-hard-primary).
Visual Direction: "Simple. Familiar. Reassuring. Slightly expressive."
