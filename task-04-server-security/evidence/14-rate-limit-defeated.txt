3. SECURITY CONCLUSION & ARCHITECTURAL IMPLICATIONS:
- An IP-only rate limit is INSUFFICIENT against distributed brute-force or attackers who can spoof/rotate IP addresses.
- IP-based limits also risk blocking benign users behind shared CGNAT, corporate proxies, or Nigerian university Wi-Fi networks.
- Effective rate limiting must combine: (1) Client IP, (2) Target Account Identifier (email/username), and (3) CAPTCHA/proof-of-work or multi-factor authentication upon suspicious velocity spikes.
- Behind reverse proxies (e.g., Cloudflare, AWS ALB), proxy configurations must strip untrusted client-supplied X-Forwarded-For headers and only trust the real connecting socket or CF-Connecting-IP.
